# Community conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss the work rather than attacking people, and help others learn. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, disclosure of private information and repeated disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate, transparent where privacy permits, and offer a route to discuss mistakes. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an established channel. A dedicated contact will be configured before public launch.
