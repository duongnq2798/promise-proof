# Open-core business proposal

**Fact:** the implementation runs locally without accounts, a hosted service or a paid model API. No adoption, revenue or willingness to pay has been validated.

**Hypothesis:** teams may pay to review evidence across contributors and releases when local folders become hard to manage. Individual deterministic runs and offline reports should remain useful without payment.

**Proposal:** retain the runner, schemas, CLI, reporters, examples and local agent workflow under MIT. If research supports it, build an optional team service for shared retention, release comparisons, organization policy, managed isolated runners and test-health analytics. Keep bundles portable and permit private storage adapters. Never make the free verdict depend on a commercial API.

**Validation before building:** recruit developers using the CLI, observe repeated verification work, measure report usefulness and failure reproduction, then test willingness to pay for a concrete collaboration problem. Define pricing after interviews/pilots. Storage, security and support costs may outweigh early revenue.

**Deferred:** billing, accounts, hosted database, schedules, enterprise access control and dashboards. None is implemented or represented as validated revenue.
